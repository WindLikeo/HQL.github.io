Hi, I am Qinlei Huang.
=====

I studied in the [School of Software](https://www.sc.sdu.edu.cn) at [Shandong University](https://www.sdu.edu.cn), under the supervision of Prof. [Liqiang Nie](https://liqiangnie.github.io/index.html) and Prof. [Yupeng Hu](https://faculty.sdu.edu.cn/huyupeng1/zh_CN/index.htm). My research interests include multimodal retrieval, robust representation learning, and trustworthy AI.
